How PepsTracker Compares Peptide Prices

PepsTracker is an independent price-comparison site. We do not sell, manufacture, or ship any product. We track what 26 research-peptide vendors publicly list, normalize every listing to cost per milligram, and show which source is cheapest for a given compound.

What the data covers

  • 26 vendors tracked
  • 83 research compounds
  • 1117 individual price listings (vendor x compound x vial size)
  • 20 discount codes, ranging from 5% to 50% off
  • Refreshed daily by an automated scraper

How we calculate cost per milligram

Vendors sell the same compound in different vial sizes, so headline price is not comparable. For every listing we compute:

cost per mg = (list price x (1 - discount)) / milligrams

Each vendor's public discount code is applied before ranking, because that is the price a buyer actually pays. For a compound sold in several vial sizes, we use the size with the lowest cost per mg. This is why the ranking can differ from a simple price sort.

Where the prices come from

Prices are read from each vendor's own public storefront - product pages, catalog feeds, or store APIs. We do not accept vendor-submitted pricing. When a vendor's site is unreachable or blocks automated access, that vendor's prices are updated manually and may lag.

Promotions and limitations

Cost per mg reflects list price with the standing discount code applied. It does not model buy-one-get-one offers, first-order-only promotions, bundle pricing, shipping, or tax. Where a vendor runs a standing BOGO, we label it on that vendor's card rather than folding it into the ranking, so every vendor is compared on the same basis.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or distribute any product.
  • We do not verify purity, potency, or sterility. A "COA tested" label means the vendor publishes certificates of analysis, not that we have independently confirmed them.
  • We do not accept payment to change a ranking. Rankings are computed from price data only.

Some vendor links are affiliate links. Affiliate status has no effect on position in any comparison - ordering is a pure cost-per-mg sort.

Corrections

If a price here is wrong, it is either stale or a scraping error. Both are worth reporting - the fastest fix is to send the vendor, compound, and the correct listing.
