Methodology. PepsTracker scans the live catalogs of 28 US research peptide vendors every day and records the best available price per milligram for each of 83 compounds, with vendor discount codes applied. Each index normalizes every compound to 100 at the first day it was tracked, then takes the daily median across compounds — so the index measures how the market moves, not which compounds happen to be expensive. The GLP-1 class covers semaglutide, tirzepatide, retatrutide and cagrilintide; repair & recovery covers BPC-157, TB-500, GHK-Cu and epithalon. Data updates automatically each day after the scan.
